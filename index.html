About Me

I'm a Finance & Business Analytics MBA graduate based in Bangalore, currently at the start of my career and actively working toward roles in equity research, investment analysis and financial markets. My coursework and independent projects have focused on reading financial statements, calculating and interpreting financial ratios, and connecting company fundamentals to how a stock actually trades.

I don't yet have professional experience in the markets — what I bring instead is a disciplined, evidence-first approach: I build my own datasets, document my sources and methodology, and try to let the numbers speak before I draw a conclusion. I'm looking for an entry-level opportunity where I can apply that approach and keep learning from people who've been doing this longer than I have.
